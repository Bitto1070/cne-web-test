# Differences between the other FNF engines.

### First of all, **this engine does not have built-in LUA scripting**, unlike <a href="https://github.com/ShadowMario/FNF-PsychEngine">Psych Engine</a>.

Codename engine is based on **Base Game's week 6 code** (AKA. version 0.2.7.1), and holds heavy modifications from the original source (for example, optimized PlayState, rewritten systems, brand new modding system made from the ground up, new editors etc.). It is not a fork of any other engines and hasn't taken code from any of them either.

Compared to the usual engines (Psych Engine, FPS plus, V-Slice), these are the few differences that makes it stand out:
- The scripting system is powered by HScript Improved, a custom fork of HScript made by Codename Crew.

- The forks of essential libraries (flixel, openfl) are tailored specifically for the engine for new functions and optimizations.

- Modding system and softcoding is NEARLY as capable as source coding (with no limitations or restrictions to what you can do in HScript).

- Many, and i mean MANY modding tools available in the base build (such as 3D, Nape physics, NDLL loading and much more).

- Mods are instance-based meaning that installed mods won't conflict with eachother and so makes them on-par with Executable Mods.

- Advanced editors which allows for quickly testing song events and saves directly to the files.

<Note>
Please keep in mind that, despite these differences, we do not consider our engine to be any better than the others and this page exists purely for people to distinguish our engine from the other engines in the community.
</Note>
